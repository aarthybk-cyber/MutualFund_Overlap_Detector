Mutual Fund Overlap Detector

Shows what a multi-fund portfolio actually holds, once you look through the labels.

Dataset · 60 funds × 36 months (118,800 rows, 350 ISINs) Status · Deployed, verified Deliverable · 1 live web app, 4 analysis views

Live app: mutualfundoverlapdetector-be4zqrjldjzvq2khkwafpb.streamlit.app  ·  Source: github.com/aarthybk-cyber/MutualFund_Overlap_Detector

The problem

Holding several funds feels diversified, but funds are just baskets of stocks — two differently-named funds can quietly hold the same companies. If they do, a downturn in that cluster hits the whole portfolio at once, and nothing on a standard fact sheet shows this.

This tool answers two questions:

  1. How much do two funds overlap? One overlap percentage per fund pair, or a heatmap across several funds at once.
  2. What do I really own? True blended stock exposure across a chosen mix of funds and allocation amounts.

What was built

01
My Portfolio
Pick funds, enter allocations and a month: overlap heatmap, top-20 merged holdings, concentration flags (>5% of corpus), and a 36-month overlap trend
02
Pairwise Overlap
Overlap between any two funds, with the largest contributing stocks
03
All Pairs & Sanity Check
Overlap for all 1,770 fund pairs in a month, automatic integrity checks, and category-level expectation checks
04
Data Checks
Underlying dataset integrity: weights sum to 100% per fund-month, no nulls, duplicates, or negative weights

All four live in one deployed web app, built with Python/pandas and Streamlit — no install needed to use it.

Verification & findings

The overlap formula matches a hand-calculated fixture exactly (65.0%), and every automated check passes: 1,770 unique fund pairs per month with no self-pairs or duplicates, every value within the valid 0–100% range, and the full Phase 1 data-integrity suite (no nulls, no duplicate rows, weights summing to 100% per fund-month). Small Cap vs Index overlap stays flat at 0% across all 36 months — comfortably under the expected ceiling.
One flagged exception, kept rather than smoothed over: Large Cap vs Large Cap overlap runs a touch below the course material's stated 55–75% benchmark across the full 36-month history (median 52%, range 31–66%). Noted here as a genuine finding for the write-up, since the point of a sanity check is catching real patterns, not producing tidy numbers.

Why it matters

This is the same check an advisor would normally do by hand, fund by fund, from monthly disclosures — here it's automatic, live, and covers the full fund universe and three years of history in seconds.
